## 📌 Project Overview
This project provides a comprehensive analysis of retail sales data to identify key drivers of profitability and growth. Using **Power BI**, I transformed raw business data into an interactive dashboard to answer critical business questions regarding regional performance, product success, and customer segments. The dashboard enables **data-driven decision making** for business strategy and growth.

---

## 🚀 Business Questions Answered
1. **Key Metrics:** Track total Sales ($2.33M), Profit ($292.30K), and Return Rate (5.79%) with Year-over-Year comparisons.  
2. **Temporal Trends:** Analyze year-over-year sales to identify seasonal growth patterns.  
3. **Product Performance:** Determine the most profitable products (Technology / Copiers) and identify underperforming products (Furniture / Tables).  
4. **Geographic Insights:** Map states where the majority of profit is generated.  
5. **Customer Segmentation:** Analyze sales distribution across Consumer, Corporate, and Home Office segments.

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Skills Demonstrated
- **Power BI:** Interactive dashboards, DAX calculations, visual storytelling  
- **Excel:** Data cleaning, preparation, and initial analysis  
- **GitHub:** Version control and portfolio hosting

---

## 📊 Key Insights
- **Profit Leak:** The Tables sub-category under Furniture shows negative profit margins, while Technology drives strong revenue, suggesting a review of pricing or suppliers.  
- **Regional Dominance:** Profit is heavily concentrated in states such as California and New York, highlighting opportunities for targeted marketing campaigns.  
- **Customer Segments:** Consumer and Corporate segments contribute most to total sales.  
- **Efficiency:** The Return Rate decreased by 2.95% compared to last year, indicating improved customer satisfaction or better product descriptions.  

---

## 📈 Recommendations
1. **Product Strategy:** Review pricing or suppliers for underperforming sub-categories like Tables to improve profitability.  
2. **Targeted Marketing:** Focus on high-profit regions (e.g., California, New York) to maximize ROI on campaigns.  
3. **Customer Engagement:** Introduce personalized promotions for Consumer and Corporate segments to increase loyalty and repeat purchases.  
4. **Inventory & Supply Planning:** Align stock levels with regional demand patterns to reduce lost sales or overstock.

---

## ✅ Conclusions
This analysis highlights **clear opportunities to increase profitability** through strategic product and regional focus. Leveraging the insights from this dashboard supports **data-driven decision making**, optimizes revenue streams, and improves operational efficiency. The dashboard provides business leaders with actionable insights to strengthen overall performance and customer satisfaction.
